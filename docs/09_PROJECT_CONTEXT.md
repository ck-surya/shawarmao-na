## Product
Restaurant-owned direct ordering website.

## Strategy
Do not try to replace Zomato/Swiggy immediately. Keep aggregators for discovery while building the restaurant's own direct/repeat-order channel.

## Acquisition ideas
QR on packaging, receipts and counter; Instagram/Google link; reorder prompts; future direct-order offers.

## V1 delivery
Restaurant-managed delivery/pickup. Third-party provider later.

## V1 payment
COD/manual payment can be used. Online payment only with proper gateway verification.

## Build philosophy
Single restaurant first. Keep code modular enough for future reuse but do not build a SaaS platform now.

## Commercial philosophy
Keep V1 focused and affordable for a first freelance project. Avoid expensive infrastructure and unnecessary complexity.

## Future
If successful, architecture can later be generalized into a reusable restaurant ordering platform.
